🐟 Master of Science Thesis in Aquaculture

Thesis Title: Evaluation of Nannochloropsis sp.-Enriched Brachionus sp. as Live Feed for Enhancing the Growth and Survival of Milkfish (Chanos chanos) Larvae
Researcher: Nusrat Nasrin
Institution: School of Graduate Studies, Department of Aquaculture, Faculty of Agriculture, Universiti Putra Malaysia (UPM)
Completion Date: February 2026

Abstract Overview: The long-term sustainability of the aquaculture sector hinges on the continuous production of healthy fish seed. This comprehensive graduate research investigates the live-feed sequence including laboratory culture of Nannochloropsis sp., production and nutritional enrichment of Brachionus sp., and its subsequent impact on enhancing the growth, survival, and nutritional profile of milkfish (Chanos chanos) larvae during early hatchery production.

The findings provide critical insights into live-feed management strategies, optimizing nutritional transfer from microalgae to rotifers and subsequently to commercial fish larvae to mitigate excessive larval mortality in tropical and subtropical hatcheries.
