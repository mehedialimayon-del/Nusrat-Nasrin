ABOUT ME

MY JOURNEY

From a determined young girl in Jessore to an Agriculturist, Aquaculturist, Researcher and Marketing professional in Malaysia — this is the story of Nusrat Nasrin's academic, professional and personal journey.

PERSONAL STORY

A Journey Built on Dreams & Determination

Nusrat Nasrin was born on 16 December 1999 in Jessore, Bangladesh, in the Chougachha area of Jessore District. On a bright and sunlit December day, her parents, Fosiyuzzaman and Aleya Khatun, welcomed their daughter into the world.

From childhood, Nusrat was energetic, courageous, determined and exceptionally curious. Her independent personality and fearless nature often made her parents feel as though they had been blessed with a son. She grew up with a strong sense of responsibility, resilience and ambition.

That determination gradually became one of the strongest characteristics of her personality. She developed a commitment not only to building her own future but also to supporting and caring for the people she loves. Today, that same sense of responsibility continues to influence the way she approaches her family, education, career and research.

Nusrat's long-term ambition is to become a highly accomplished researcher and scientist, develop expertise in aquaculture and aquatic science, achieve higher academic qualifications and contribute meaningful knowledge to sustainable aquatic production and research.

01

Early Education

Her educational journey began in Bangladesh, where she successfully completed her primary, secondary and higher secondary education. Throughout her student life, she remained serious about education while maintaining an active and confident personality.

02

Choosing Fisheries

After completing her school and college education, Nusrat chose Fisheries as her academic field because of her genuine interest in aquatic resources, fisheries science and the potential of aquaculture.

03

Bangladesh Agricultural University

She entered Bangladesh Agricultural University in 2018 and joined the Fisheries Faculty. Her choice reflected a clear academic interest in fisheries, aquaculture and aquatic science. She successfully completed her Bachelor's degree with dedication and strong academic commitment.

04

Leadership & Campus Life

University life developed her leadership, teamwork, communication and organizational abilities. She was actively involved in student and cultural activities, took leadership responsibilities and built strong relationships with classmates, seniors and juniors.

AGRICULTURIST

Bangladesh Agricultural University

Bangladesh Agricultural University became one of the most important foundations of Nusrat's academic and professional identity. Her decision to study Fisheries was driven by a genuine interest in aquatic resources and the desire to eventually work in research and sustainable aquaculture.

Her university years were not limited to classroom learning. They helped her develop scientific thinking, discipline, communication, leadership, teamwork and the confidence to work with people from different backgrounds.

Fisheries Faculty

Fisheries was her first choice because of her interest in aquatic science. The academic environment introduced her to fisheries biology, aquatic ecosystems, fish production, aquaculture, aquatic resource management, fisheries economics and the scientific principles behind sustainable aquatic production.

Academic Discipline

Her university education strengthened her analytical ability and scientific mindset. Practical learning, laboratory exposure, field-oriented education and academic teamwork helped prepare her for future research.

Leadership

Nusrat became actively involved in university life, student organizations and cultural activities. These experiences developed her leadership, responsibility, public communication and ability to coordinate with different people.

Sultana Razia Hall

Her residential life at Sultana Razia Hall became another important part of her university journey. The experience combined friendship, study, discipline, senior-junior relationships and everyday campus memories, creating a strong sense of community and independence.

OVERSEAS EDUCATION

From Bangladesh to Malaysia

A New Chapter in Malaysia

After completing her university journey in Bangladesh, Nusrat chose Malaysia as the next destination for her higher education and professional development. In 2024, she moved to Malaysia with her spouse, Mehedi Alim Ayon, with the ambition of creating a stronger future through education, professional experience and research.

Although her ultimate academic goal was research in aquaculture, she also had a strong interest in business and management. Therefore, she decided to pursue an MBA in Malaysia while keeping her long-term research ambition firmly in sight.

Her MBA journey gave her exposure to business strategy, management, marketing, organizational behaviour, communication, leadership and decision-making. These skills later became valuable in her professional career as well as in her research journey.

After completing her Master's/MBA within approximately one year, she moved toward her primary academic interest: aquaculture research at Universiti Putra Malaysia (UPM).

BUSINESS & MANAGEMENT

Strategic thinking, marketing, management, organizational leadership, communication and business decision-making.

AQUACULTURE

Aquatic production, sustainable aquaculture, aquatic resources, fish production and modern approaches to aquatic science.

RESEARCH MINDSET

Scientific observation, experimentation, analysis, academic writing and the pursuit of meaningful research outcomes.

AQUACULTURIST & RESEARCHER

Research at UPM

Universiti Putra Malaysia has become the next major chapter in Nusrat's academic journey. Her decision to return to her core field of aquaculture reflects a clear long-term vision: to develop herself as a researcher and eventually become a scientist with meaningful contributions to aquatic science.

Her current research direction is connected with aquaculture, aquatic science and sustainable aquatic innovation. She is working toward developing strong research skills, preparing academic work and pursuing journal publication opportunities.

Aquaculture Research

Her research interest focuses on modern aquaculture, aquatic production and sustainable approaches that can contribute to the future development of aquatic food systems.

Microalgae & Innovation

Her academic interests also connect with microalgae, biotechnology and innovative aquatic applications, providing opportunities to explore new solutions within aquaculture research.

Scientific Development

Through research, laboratory work, academic discussion and scientific analysis, Nusrat is continuously developing the skills required for advanced research.

Future PhD Goal

Her long-term academic goal is to continue toward a PhD and establish herself as a professional researcher and scientist in aquaculture and aquatic science.
