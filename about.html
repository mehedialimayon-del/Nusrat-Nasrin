MY STORY

A Journey of Purpose & Ambition

Nusrat Nasrin's journey is a story of determination, academic excellence, family responsibility and continuous growth. From her early life in Jashore to Bangladesh Agricultural University, and eventually to Malaysia for higher education and research, every stage of her life has contributed to the professional she is becoming today.

♙

Early Life

Nusrat Nasrin was born on 16 December 1999 in Jashore, Bangladesh, and belongs to Chaugachha Upazila of Jashore District. She was born into a loving family to her father, Fosiyuzzaman, and mother, Aleya Khatun. From childhood, Nusrat was known for her energetic, courageous and determined personality. Her independent nature and fearless attitude made her stand out from an early age.

★

A Determined Personality

Her family saw in her a child who was exceptionally active, talented and ambitious. She developed a strong sense of responsibility from an early age and gradually became a person her family could depend on. Her ambition has always been larger than circumstances, and today she continues to carry that same determination into her academic and professional life.

✦

Family, Responsibility & Vision

One of the strongest parts of Nusrat's personality is her commitment to her family. She has always wanted to become financially independent, support her family and build a meaningful future through her own abilities. Her long-term vision is to establish herself as a highly skilled researcher and scientist while continuing to take responsibility for the people she loves. Her journey reflects a combination of academic ambition, personal courage, independence and responsibility.

ACADEMIC JOURNEY

From Bangladesh to Malaysia

EARLY

School & College Education

Nusrat successfully completed her primary, secondary and higher-secondary education with strong academic performance and developed a clear interest in science, agriculture, fisheries and aquatic resources.

2018

Bangladesh Agricultural University

After completing her higher-secondary education, Nusrat entered Bangladesh Agricultural University. Fisheries was her first choice because of her deep interest in aquatic science and her ambition to work in research related to fisheries and aquaculture.

BAU LIFE

Fisheries Faculty & Campus Life

During her university years, Nusrat became an active and familiar face within the Fisheries community. Her academic seriousness was complemented by leadership, communication, teamwork and involvement with student and cultural activities. Her hall life at Sultana Razia Hall also contributed to her discipline, friendships, teamwork and understanding of senior-junior relationships.

2024

Overseas Education — Malaysia

After completing her undergraduate journey in Bangladesh, Nusrat chose Malaysia for higher education. Her decision was influenced by her desire for international exposure, professional development and ultimately returning to her core interest in aquaculture research.

MBA

Business & Management Education

Alongside her scientific interests, Nusrat developed a strong interest in business and management. She completed her MBA at Kuala Lumpur University of Science and Technology, strengthening her knowledge of management, communication, leadership, marketing and organizational strategy.

UPM

Aquaculture Research

Following her MBA, Nusrat returned to her core scientific field and began her research journey in Aquaculture at Universiti Putra Malaysia. Her current direction focuses on aquatic science, sustainable aquaculture, research development and scientific knowledge with the long-term goal of progressing toward a PhD and establishing herself as a scientist.
