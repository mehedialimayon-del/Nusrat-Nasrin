ABOUT ME

Nusrat Nasrin

Agriculturist • Aquaculturist • Researcher • Head of Marketing

A Journey Built on Courage, Curiosity & Purpose

Nusrat Nasrin was born on 16 December 1999 in Jessore, Bangladesh, in the Chougachha area of Jessore District. She grew up as an energetic, ambitious and determined young woman whose curiosity and courage were visible from an early age.

Born to FosiuZZaman and Aleya Khatun, Nusrat became known within her family for her independent spirit, strong character and willingness to take responsibility. Her journey has been shaped by a simple principle: work hard, keep learning and create a meaningful future through knowledge and perseverance.

Her long-term ambition is to develop herself as a researcher and scientist in aquaculture while continuing to support her family and contribute to sustainable aquatic development.

Academic Journey

Bangladesh Agricultural University BSc in Fisheries. A foundation in fisheries science, aquaculture, aquatic resources and practical scientific education.
University Life Academic discipline combined with leadership, organizational activities, cultural involvement and teamwork.
Malaysia Moved overseas in 2024 to pursue higher education and expand her professional and academic horizon.
Master / MBA Completed an MBA in Malaysia, developing her understanding of business, management, communication and leadership.
Universiti Putra Malaysia Currently pursuing research in Aquaculture with a strong interest in sustainable aquatic science and innovation.
Future Goal Build a strong research career, publish scientific work and progress towards a PhD and a career as a scientist.

Life at Bangladesh Agricultural University

Bangladesh Agricultural University provided the environment where Nusrat developed her academic identity in Fisheries. Her interest in aquatic science made Fisheries a natural first choice, and her university years became an important chapter in her development.

Leadership & Campus Life

Beyond academics, she participated in university organizations, cultural activities and student initiatives. These experiences strengthened her communication ability, teamwork, leadership, discipline and confidence.

Sultana Razia Hall

Her residential life at Sultana Razia Hall added another dimension to her university experience. Hall life brought together study, friendship, senior-junior relationships, discipline, cultural activities and everyday memories that helped shape her personality.

Life in Malaysia

In 2024, Nusrat chose Malaysia as the next chapter of her educational and professional journey. She came to Malaysia together with her spouse, Mehedi Alim Ayon, with the intention of building an independent future while continuing higher education.

Her main academic ambition remained research in aquaculture. At the same time, she pursued an MBA because of her interest in business, management and organizational leadership.

Professional Growth

Alongside her academic journey, Nusrat entered the travel and professional services sector. What began as an opportunity to gain experience developed into a significant professional pathway.

Her communication skills, discipline, hard work, customer relationship ability and leadership qualities helped her take on greater responsibilities. Within a relatively short period, she progressed into a leadership role in marketing.

Head of Marketing

Today, she combines academic ambition with professional leadership, bringing together research, communication, business understanding and people management.

Mehedi Alim Ayon

Nusrat's journey in Malaysia is also connected with her spouse, Mehedi Alim Ayon. They first became acquainted during their university life and later chose to build their future together in Malaysia.

Mehedi is professionally involved in account management and agriculture-related work. Their shared academic background and professional ambitions have made their overseas journey a story of mutual support, growth and perseverance.
