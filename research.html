Thesis Title: Evaluation of Nannochloropsis sp.-Enriched Brachionus sp. as Live Feed for Enhancing the Growth and Survival of Milkfish (Chanos chanos) Larvae
Researcher: Nusrat Nasrin | Institution: Universiti Putra Malaysia (UPM) (February 2026)

Deep Dive into Research Methodology: Global commercial hatcheries face heavy economic losses due to high larval mortality during the critical phase of mouth-opening and initial exogenous feeding. Nusrat’s research tackles this by engineering a robust nutritional chain:

1. Microalgae Mass Production

Cultivating Nannochloropsis sp. to harness high essential fatty acid profiles (EPA) and vital proteins, acting as the primary biological nutrient source.

2. Bio-Encapsulation & Rotifers

Using Brachionus sp. as a bio-vector, enriching them with the microalgae to transfer maximum nutritional value directly to the predator fish larvae.

🌍 Future Scope, Sectors & Industry Innovations

The evaluation protocols and findings developed in this UPM study hold immense value for the future of global aquaculture, aligning directly with international food security and Blue Economy initiatives.

Commercial Hatchery Sector

Directly resolves early-stage larval mortality issues in milkfish and other high-value marine finfish, guaranteeing higher seed survival rates and profitability for commercial hatcheries.

Biotechnology & Feed Industries

Paves the way for advanced commercial microalgal paste production and specialized live-feed enrichment products tailored for tropical aquaculture markets across Asia.
