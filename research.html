🎓 Academic Evolution: BAU to Kuala Lumpur

Nusrat Nasrin’s academic foundation began on the lush, 1,250-acre green campus of **Bangladesh Agricultural University (BAU)**, where she earned stellar achievements in Fisheries (2018–2024). Driven by a lifelong dream to excel internationally and empowered by self-reliance, she moved to Malaysia to expand her global footprint.

Drawing inspiration from her family's business background, she pursued and successfully completed a intensive **1-year Master of Business Administration (MBA) at the Kuala Lumpur University of Science and Technology**.

Impact on Research & Industry: In modern science, pure research must merge with economic feasibility. This MBA empowers Nusrat to bridge scientific innovations (like hatcheries and live-feed optimization) with commercial scalability, project budgeting, and agricultural entrepreneurship, ensuring her research delivers high commercial and social ROI.

Thesis Title: Evaluation of Nannochloropsis sp.-Enriched Brachionus sp. as Live Feed for Enhancing the Growth and Survival of Milkfish (Chanos chanos) Larvae
Researcher: Nusrat Nasrin | Institution: Universiti Putra Malaysia (UPM) (February 2026)

Problem & Methodology: High larval mortality in hatcheries stems from undeveloped digestive tracts and poor nutritional profiles in standard starter feeds. This research formulates an integrated live-feed pathway: cultivating microalgae Nannochloropsis sp. as a nutrient bio-producer, bio-transferring essential fatty acids (EPA) and proteins into rotifers (Brachionus sp.), and feeding them to milkfish (Chanos chanos) larvae under a Completely Randomized Design (CRD).

🌍 Future Innovations & Global Industry Applications

The findings from this advanced research open vast horizons for commercial hatcheries and sustainable aquaculture development across Southeast Asia and global markets.

Commercial Hatchery Optimization

Provides standardized protocols for microalgal enrichment, directly solving excessive larval mortality and boosting juvenile seed production yields for milkfish and other high-value marine species.

Blue Economy & Food Security

Aligns with FAO's Blue Transformation initiatives by substituting low-quality artificial feeds with nutrient-dense biological vectors, minimizing environmental footprint while maximizing profitability.

Future Research & Biotechnological Expansion

Future phases will explore automated microalgal photobioreactors, multi-strain rotifer bio-encapsulation, and gene-expression profiling in fish larvae under stress conditions. Blending her scientific expertise with her MBA background, Nusrat aims to lead large-scale commercial biotechnology projects and aquaculture consultancy worldwide.
